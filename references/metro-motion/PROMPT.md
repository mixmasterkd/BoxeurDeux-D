# Panoramas des fenêtres du métro

Source créée avec la génération d’images intégrée; références : wagon original et wagon à quai. Les poignées, encadrements et barres viennent toujours du wagon original. Extraction technique reproductible : `scripts/prepare-metro-windows.mjs`.

Create a game texture atlas with TWO separate horizontal panorama strips of equal height, stacked vertically, 1536 x 1024 canvas. This is background scenery to scroll OUTSIDE the subway carriage windows of this existing 16-bit Montreal-inspired pixel art game. The reference images show the carriage ONLY for palette/style/context. DO NOT draw any part of the carriage: no window frames, no yellow poles, no hanging handles, no train doors, no UI, no letters or text. TOP HALF (y=0..511): uninterrupted side view of the opposite station platform wall: warm cream small ceramic tiles with blue and orange horizontal stripes, spaced blue pillars with warm rectangular lights, a few complete blue benches on a narrow strip of tiled platform ground at the bottom. Nearly orthographic side-on view with a little height visible on the bench seats, harmonious detailed hand-painted 16-bit pixel art, sharp pixel clusters. Identical tile rows at the left and right edges so it repeats horizontally; benches and pillars must not touch the left/right image edges. BOTTOM HALF (y=512..1023): an uninterrupted side-on underground subway tunnel wall, dark blue grey masonry, utility cables and spaced small warm rectangular lamps. Matching visual scale and horizon to upper strip. Lower half must tile horizontally too, leave plain masonry at both edges. Each panorama fills its WHOLE half with no margins or frames. Station fills upper half exactly, tunnel fills lower half exactly; no gradient between halves, no sky. Keep the original scene's charming warm/cool 16-bit palette and crisp textured pixels, not photorealism. These are empty surroundings without people or any train. A horizontal hard separation exactly at middle height is necessary for technical extraction of two equal-height textures.
